This demo shows what it looks like to operate a subscription business by talking to it: an AI agent with direct access to Shopify, Recharge, and Klaviyo — asking real questions, getting real answers, making real changes. Live. No slides pretending to be software.

What happens live

Three moves, all in plain English, all against live APIs — the same questions a subscription operator asks every Monday morning.

ASK — "What happened to the Black Friday cohort?"
The agent audits 14 months of order history and the Recharge cancellation records, finds the 60-subscriber churn cliff from the bf-promo campaign, and explains why the discount cohort behaved differently from full-price subscribers.
CHANGE — "Build me a winback segment and make it worth their while."
Real mutations, not mockups: a customer segment in Shopify, a discount in Recharge, an email flow through Klaviyo — created in front of the audience by asking for them.
SHIP — "Put a subscription pitch on the storefront."
The agent writes and publishes a theme section that sells the subscription to the 61% — closing the loop from insight to storefront in one session.

The point

"I didn't know I could build this with Shopify."

Shopify and Recharge give you the primitives — orders, contracts, charges, segments, themes. AI is the operator that composes them. The gap between "I have a question about my business" and "it's fixed and live" collapses to a conversation. That's what this workshop is trying to make people feel first-hand.
